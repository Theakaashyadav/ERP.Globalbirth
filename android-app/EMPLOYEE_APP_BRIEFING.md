# Global One Employee App

## Employee Briefing and User Guide

This document explains the Global One Employee App, its features, company rules, and the correct way to use it. Employees should read this guide before using the app.

---

## 1. Purpose of the App

The Global One Employee App is used for:

- Employee login and identity verification
- Daily attendance
- Employee profile information
- Sales lead assignment and follow-up
- Lead call tracking
- Follow-up and meeting reminders
- Company alerts and notifications
- Mandatory application updates

The features shown on the dashboard depend on the employee’s department, designation, and access approved by the administrator.

---

## 2. Who Can Use the App

Only registered and active employees can log in.

The company currently uses these departments:

- Sales
- HR
- Backend

Sales employees can have one of these designations:

- TL — Team Leader
- Executive

Every Sales Executive must be placed under an active Sales TL by HR.

### Typical feature access

| Employee type | Available features |
|---|---|
| Sales TL | Attendance, assigned leads, team leads, alerts, profile and sign out |
| Sales Executive | Attendance, assigned leads, alerts, profile and sign out |
| HR employee | Attendance, profile and sign out, subject to administrator settings |
| Backend employee | Attendance, profile and sign out, subject to administrator settings |

The administrator can enable or disable individual mobile features for TLs and Executives. Therefore, an employee may not see every feature listed above.

---

## 3. First-Time Registration

1. Open the app and select **Register**.
2. Enter the requested personal information carefully.
3. Enter a valid 10-digit mobile number.
4. Create a 4-digit PIN that you can remember but other people cannot guess.
5. Submit the form.
6. Registration does not immediately activate the account.
7. Wait for HR to verify the information, select the department and designation, and change the account status to **Active**.

An inactive employee cannot log in.

### Important registration rules

- Use your own mobile number.
- Do not register duplicate accounts.
- Do not share your PIN.
- The account becomes linked to the Android phone used for the first approved login.
- Contact HR if your registered phone is lost, replaced, formatted, or repaired.

---

## 4. Login and Biometric Login

The employee login ID is the registered mobile number. The PIN is always four digits.

### PIN login

1. Enter the registered mobile number.
2. Enter the 4-digit PIN.
3. Select **Login**.

The app remembers the login ID on the device.

### Biometric login

If the phone supports biometrics and the employee has previously logged in successfully, the app shows biometric login first.

- Authenticate using the phone’s fingerprint or supported biometric method.
- Select **Use PIN** if biometric authentication is unavailable or unsuccessful.
- On phones without biometric support, the PIN login is shown directly.

Biometric authentication confirms the person using the registered device. It does not permit the account to be used on another phone.

### Device protection

Login is checked using:

- Registered mobile number
- Correct 4-digit PIN or approved biometric authentication
- Active employee status
- Registered Android device identity

Sharing a mobile number and PIN with another person will not allow that person to log in from another phone.

---

## 5. Permissions Required by the App

The app requests required permissions when it is opened for the first time.

| Permission | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Notifications | New lead, follow-up, pending call and update alerts |
| Call logs | Match calls with assigned leads and calculate attempts and duration |
| Location | Support attendance verification where required |
| Wi-Fi/network information | Confirm connection to the approved office Wi-Fi before attendance |
| Install unknown apps | Install mandatory company updates without the Play Store |

Employees should approve the required permissions for their assigned features to work correctly.

The app does not continuously save the employee’s complete call history in the company database. Call-log summaries are fetched from the registered phone when an authorized dashboard requests them.

## 6. Dashboard

After successful login, the employee dashboard displays:

- Employee name
- Employee ID
- Department and designation
- Cards for the features approved for that employee role
- Notification badges for new leads and pending alerts
- Footer navigation for frequently used pages

Select a dashboard card or footer icon to open that feature.

Badge counts change as tasks are completed. For example, calling a newly assigned lead removes that lead from the new-lead count.

## 7. Attendance

### Attendance rules

- Attendance can be marked only after connecting to the approved office Wi-Fi.
- Mark attendance using your own registered phone.
- Attendance can be marked once for the day.
- The server records the official date and time using India time.
- Employees cannot edit attendance remarks.
- HR can review and update the attendance status or remark.

### How to mark attendance

1. Reach the office.
2. Connect the registered phone to the approved office Wi-Fi.
3. Open **Attendance** from the dashboard or footer.
4. Confirm that the attendance verification is successful.
5. Select **Mark My Attendance**.
6. Wait for the success message.

Do not repeatedly press the attendance button. If attendance has already been marked, the app will not create another entry for the same day.

### Attendance statuses managed by HR

- Present
- Late
- Absent
- Leave
- Other remark, where applicable

Employees should contact HR if an attendance entry is missing or incorrect.

## 9. Sales Lead Workflow

The following sections apply to Sales TLs and Sales Executives.

### Lead assignment flow

1. Marketing assigns a new lead to a Sales TL.
2. The TL receives a push notification and a new-lead badge.
3. Within 30 minutes, the TL must do one of the following:
   - Call the lead personally, or
   - Assign the lead to an Executive, who must call before the original 30-minute deadline.
4. A valid call within the deadline secures the lead.
5. After the TL personally secures a lead, the TL may keep it or assign it to an Executive at any later time.
6. If nobody calls the lead before the 30-minute deadline, the lead is automatically removed from the employee app and returned to Marketing as an unassigned lead.

Assigning a lead to an Executive is optional. A TL may handle the lead personally.

### New-lead notification

The notification displays:

- Heading: **New Lead**
- Description: **Call Within 30 Minutes**
- A call action for directly starting the phone call

Opening or viewing a lead does not secure it. A recorded call attempt is required.

## 10. TL Lead List

The TL lead screen contains three sections:

### New Leads

Shows leads that:

- Were newly assigned to the TL
- Have not been called by the TL
- Have not been assigned to an Executive

These leads require immediate attention because of the 30-minute rule.

### My Leads

Shows leads being handled by the TL, including leads the TL has already contacted and secured.

### Team Leads

This section provides a list of Executives under the TL. Select an Executive to see the leads currently assigned to that person.

The TL can open a lead from any section to view its details, call history, follow-up information and remarks.

---

## 11. Assigning a Lead to an Executive

Only the TL responsible for the lead can assign it to an Executive in that TL’s team.

1. Open the lead details.
2. Find the Executive assignment section.
3. Select an active Executive from the list.
4. Confirm the assignment.
5. The Executive receives a new-lead notification.

An Executive from another TL’s team cannot be selected.

If the lead has not yet been secured, the Executive must call before the original 30-minute deadline. Assignment does not restart the timer.

## 12. Lead Details Page

The Lead Details page contains the information required to work on a lead:

- Lead name and phone number
- Lead status
- Assignment information
- Call-attempt information
- Call duration
- Follow-up requirement
- Previous follow-up remarks
- Next follow-up or meeting date
- TL assignment controls, where permitted

Available actions include:

- **Call** — opens the phone dialer
- **WhatsApp** — opens a WhatsApp conversation with the lead
- **Assign to Executive** — available to the responsible TL
- **Update Follow-up** — saves the latest outcome, date and mandatory remark

Employees must confirm that the number belongs to the displayed lead before calling or sending a WhatsApp message.

## 13. Call Tracking Rules

The app matches assigned lead numbers with calls in the registered phone’s call log.

For each matched attempt, the system can record:

- Connected or not connected
- Call date and time
- Call duration
- Number of attempts

### When the lead is not connected

- Three call attempts per day are mandatory.
- Attempts must continue for four days unless the lead becomes connected or company instructions change.
- Alerts show how many attempts remain for the day.

### After the lead is connected

- At least one follow-up call is required within every 48-hour period, subject to the saved follow-up plan.

Employees must make calls from the registered phone so that attempts can be matched correctly.

---

## 14. Follow-up Status and Remarks

After speaking with or attempting to contact a lead, update the lead using one of these statuses:

- Interested
- Not Interested
- No Response
- Cold
- Hot
- Wrong No.
- Meeting Fix

### Date requirements

For these statuses, the next follow-up date is mandatory:

- Interested
- Cold
- Hot
- No Response

For **Meeting Fix**, the meeting date is mandatory.

### Remark requirement

A remark is mandatory for every follow-up update.

A useful remark should briefly state:

- What the lead said
- Any objection or requirement
- What was promised
- The next action
- Relevant timing or meeting details

Example:

> Interested in the service. Requested pricing details on WhatsApp. Call again Friday afternoon after reviewing the proposal.

Do not enter remarks such as “done”, “ok” or “called” because they do not help the next employee or manager understand the conversation.

Every follow-up remark is retained in history. New remarks do not replace previous remarks. The history can be reviewed by the employee, TL and authorized Marketing users.

## 15. Alerts and Reminders

The Alerts page can be opened from:

- The dashboard alert card
- The footer alert icon

Alerts can include:

- Follow-up due today
- Remaining call attempts for a lead
- New lead assignment
- Overdue connected-lead follow-up

Standard reminder times include:

- 12:00 PM
- 3:00 PM
- 6:00 PM

The alert badge appears on the dashboard, footer and app icon where supported by the phone launcher.

When the required task is completed, its count is removed. If two leads are pending and one is completed, the count decreases from two to one.

## 16. Messages in the App

The app uses short popup messages for:

- Successful actions
- Errors
- Warnings
- Missing required information

Read the complete message before retrying. Repeatedly pressing a button during a network error can create confusion even when duplicate records are prevented.

## 17. Mandatory App Updates

The company distributes Android updates directly through the app without the Play Store.

### Update process

1. Log in normally.
2. The dashboard opens.
3. If a newer mandatory version is available, an update popup appears after a short delay.
4. Select **Update App** or **Download and Update**.
5. Allow installation from this source if Android requests permission.
6. Complete the Android installation process.
7. Reopen the app.

The mandatory update popup cannot be closed to continue using an outdated version.

Never install an APK received from an unknown person or messaging group. Use only the update displayed inside the official app.

---

## 18. Sign Out

Use **Sign Out** when:

- Another authorized person needs to use the phone for a different company account
- HR instructs you to sign in again
- Login information appears incorrect
- The app is being handed over with the company phone

Signing out does not transfer the employee account to another Android phone.

## 19. Privacy and Responsible Use

- Employee credentials are personal and must not be shared.
- Lead phone numbers and remarks are confidential company information.
- Do not copy lead information into unauthorized applications or personal records.
- Use lead phone numbers only for approved company communication.
- Call-log access is used to match assigned lead calls and provide requested work summaries.
- HR and authorized Marketing users may request real-time call statistics from the registered phone.
- Employees must not modify, disable or bypass app permissions required for assigned work.
- Report a lost or stolen registered phone to HR immediately.

## 20. Troubleshooting

### “Cannot connect to attendance server”

1. Confirm that mobile data or Wi-Fi is working.
2. Confirm that the phone can access the company network/server.
3. Disable and re-enable Wi-Fi once.
4. Reopen the app and retry.
5. If the issue continues, report the exact message, employee ID, phone model and time of the error to the technical team.

### “Employee account is inactive”

Contact HR. Registration alone does not activate an account.

### “Device verification failed”

The account is registered to another Android device or the phone identity changed. Contact HR for device verification or reset. Do not create another employee account.

### Biometric login is unavailable

- Confirm that a fingerprint or supported biometric is configured in Android settings.
- Use **Use PIN** to log in.
- Complete one successful PIN login before expecting biometric login.

### Attendance cannot be marked

- Confirm connection to the approved office Wi-Fi.
- Approve the required location and network permissions.
- Check whether attendance has already been marked.
- Contact HR if the issue continues.

### A lead disappeared

A new lead that was not called or delegated and called within 30 minutes is automatically returned to Marketing. Contact the TL or Marketing manager if reassignment is required.

### Calls are not appearing

- Allow Call Log permission.
- Make the call from the registered phone.
- Confirm that the phone number matches the assigned lead.
- Return to the lead page and allow the app time to synchronize.

### Notifications are not appearing

- Allow notification permission.
- Check Android notification settings for Global One.
- Disable battery restrictions for the app if notifications are heavily delayed.
- Confirm that the employee account is active and logged in.

### Mandatory update cannot install

- Allow **Install unknown apps** for Global One.
- Ensure enough storage is available.
- Keep the server/network connection active while downloading.
- Retry from the mandatory update screen.

## 21. Daily Employee Checklist

### All employees

- Log in using your registered phone.
- Connect to approved office Wi-Fi.
- Mark attendance.
- Review dashboard alerts.
- Keep notifications enabled.
- Complete mandatory app updates.

### Sales Executives

- Check new assigned leads immediately.
- Call new leads within the remaining 30-minute deadline.
- Complete the required daily call attempts.
- Enter the correct follow-up status.
- Enter a meaningful mandatory remark.
- Save the required follow-up or meeting date.
- Review alerts at 12 PM, 3 PM and 6 PM.

### Sales TLs

- Review new leads immediately.
- Call or delegate each new lead within 30 minutes.
- Monitor leads assigned to every Executive.
- Review pending attempts, statuses and remarks.
- Ensure Executives enter meaningful follow-up information.
- Follow up on alerts and overdue tasks.
